COSTING CASE STUDY

Coffee Cart Cost Estimation

A sample commercial costing exercise that converts a concept into a traceable BOQ, direct cost, overhead, contingency and recommended selling price using publicly available supplier pricing as the benchmark.

DIRECT COST฿7,783.80
TOTAL COST฿9,729.75
QUOTE฿14,000
GROSS MARGIN≈ 30.5%

1. Market Benchmark

Comparable commercial carts and publicly listed material prices establish the starting point before any custom adjustment is made.

2. Cost Build-Up

Steel frame, top, cladding, casters, finishing, signage, fabrication labour, consumables and delivery are separated into traceable line items.

3. Commercial Layer

Project overhead and contingency are added before applying the target gross margin, so profit is not confused with project operating cost.

Market Benchmark→Quantity Take-Off→Supplier Cost→Labour→Overhead→Margin→Selling Price

Portfolio sample only. Public supplier prices are used for cost-estimation methodology; final quotations require confirmed specifications, quantities, supplier quotations and project conditions.
